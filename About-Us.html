About Us

Knowledge, Humility, Character (विद्या-विनय-शील)

The Saraswati Vidyalaya and Junior College (Arts & Science) in Bhivapur Ward, Chandrapur, was founded in 1984 by Late Shri. Sudhakarraoji Potdukhe, the Chairman of Vidarbha Gramvikas Shikshan Mandal Chandrapur with the motto 'Knowledge, Humility, Character' (विद्या-विनय-शील). The school aimed to bring education to a historically backward area.

Our Journey of Growth

1984: Foundation

Established to provide quality education in a backward region, opening doors for local communities.

1992: Expansion

Introduced Junior College (Arts stream) to bridge higher education gaps for rural students.

Today: Modern Integration

Added computer labs and extracurriculars like Scouts, Guides, and Cadet Corps for holistic development.

Embracing Tomorrow

The school’s philosophy emphasizes not only academic achievement but also the development of strong, cultured citizens. It instills discipline and refinement through activities such as Scouts, Guides, and the Cadet Corps. Keeping pace with the modern world, the institution has established a separate computer lab that provides essential technical education to its students.
